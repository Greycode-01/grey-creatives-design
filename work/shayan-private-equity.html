Shayan Private Equity

A restrained identity for a private equity house

Sector
Finance
What we designed
Logo & identity, Print & stationery
In the gallery
4 images

The brief

Shayan is a fund management and equity finance brand that needed to look established from day one, with trust and growth built into the first impression.

The identity

We drew a mark that reads as both an S and a rising chart, set in navy with a gold arrow. It sits on a restrained set of stationery: letterhead and business cards that feel like an established firm.
