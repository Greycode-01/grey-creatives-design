Zavron Group

Signage, stationery and packaging across the Zavron family

Sector
Trade & logistics
What we designed
Logo & identity, Signage, Packaging, Print & stationery
In the gallery
4 images

The brief

Zavron is a trade and distribution business operating across Nigeria and Ghana. It needed one parent identity strong enough to sit beside its sister brands, Zavia and Zuria.

The identity

We built a Z mark of arrows in motion, with the line Connecting African Commerce, and carried it onto warehouse signage, office glass, packaging and business cards that also introduce the sister brands.
