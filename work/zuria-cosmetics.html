Work/Zuria Cosmetics

Zuria Cosmetics

Soft packaging and a campaign look for a cosmetics line

Sector
Cosmetics
What we designed
Logo & identity, Packaging, Social & campaign graphics
In the gallery
4 images

The brief

Zuria is a skincare and cosmetics line, another sister brand under Zavron Group. It needed a clean, botanical identity that feels like clean luxury on a shelf and on a screen.

The identity

We built the identity around a lotus mark in forest green and soft gold, then carried it across product jars, shopping bags, a perfume bottle and a campaign banner.
