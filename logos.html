How a logo comes together.

The same four steps we use for full identities, kept short.

  1. 1

    Listen

    We start with your business, your customers and the brands around you, so the work is built on the right brief.

  2. 2

    Explore

    We sketch routes for the mark and the look, and show you the strongest ideas with a clear reason behind each.

  3. 3

    Refine

    Together we sharpen one direction, then build out the colour, type and the pieces you need.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    You receive clean final files and the mockups, ready for print, signage and screen.
